**Core Principles of Technical Writing**

**1. Clarity and Directness:**

- [ ]  
    
    1.1 Use simple and precise words.
    
    1.2 Avoid long, complex sentences with multiple sub-clauses. Use the Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) structure.
    
    1.3 Every sentence should have **one** clear meaning.
    
    1.4 Define all **abbreviations and acronyms** at their first occurrence in the text (**Define on First Use**).
    

**Avoid:** "**RRM** is necessary for 6G."

**Use:** "**Radio Resource Management (RRM)** is necessary for 6G."

**2. Concise:** Cut the Clutter

- [ ]  
    
    2.1 Delete unnecessary words.
    
    2.2 Avoid "filler" phrases that do not add information.
    
    2.3 Use short sentences (easier to understand).
    

**Avoid:** "**It is important to note that** the system is fast."

**Use:** "The system is fast."

**3. Objectivity:** Facts over Feelings

- [ ]  
    
    3.1 Focus on data and facts.
    
    3.2 Avoid emotional language or personal opinions/words.
    
    3.3 Use a formal and neutral tone.
    

**Avoid:** "Our **novel** algorithm shows **big** improvement."

**Use:** "The proposed algorithm **reduces latency by 20%**."

**4. Consistency:** Use One Term

- [ ]  
    
    4.1 Use the **same terminology** throughout the paper.
    
    4.2 Do not use different words for the same concept.
    
    4.3 Use the **same text** shown on your caption.
    

**Avoid:** Referring to your method as "**the system**" in one paragraph and "**the framework**" in the next.

**Use:** Pick one (e.g., "the framework") and use it throughout the entire paper.

**5. Logical Structure:**

- [ ]  
    
    5.1 Follow the standard format (IMRaD: Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion).
    
    5.2 Start from big to small.
    
    5.3 Ensure a smooth transition between paragraphs.
    
    5.4 Ensure correlation between successive sentences/paragraphs.
    

**6. Accuracy:**

- [ ]  
    
    6.1 Check all data, citations, and formulas.
    
    6.2 Ensure that **your claims are supported by evidence**.

Key Style Tips for Students

| **Principle** | **Instead of...** | **Use...** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directness | "A study was conducted by **us**..." | "**We** conducted a study..." |
| Precision | "The result was **very big**." | "The result increased by **45%**." |
| Brevity | "... due to the fact that..." | "... because..." |
| Objectivity | "We believe this is a novel idea." | "This method differs from [1] in X and Y." |
| Brevity | "In order to provide a solution..." | "To solve..." |
| Active Voice | "The data was collected by us." | "We collected the data." |
| Precision | "The temperature was high." | "The temperature was 95°C." |
