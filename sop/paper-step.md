Steps for writing your paper:

Step 1: Copy the Template for Paper Submission and create one for yourself.

Step 2: List your contribution at the end of Sec. I

Step 3: List the scenarios used to demonstrate your results in Sec. IV. For each scenario,

Write down the goal (should be in line with the scope of your contribution, use one or more scenarios to demonstrate your contribution)

List the constants and variables to be used in this scenario

Show us the figure (X axis, Y axis, expected results)

Step 4: Draw a figure to show the system architecture/model considered in this paper

Step 5: List the reference and summarize each paper in Sec. I. For each paper,

List the paper following the format defined in IEEE Referencing Style Sheet

Briefly summarize the contribution of the paper related to your work (what has been done related to your work?)

Briefly summarize the limitation of the paper (why we need your work?)

Step 5: Write down your system architecture/model and your proposed method

Steps for revising the paper:

Step 1: Copy Template for Reply to Reviewers' Comments and create one for yourself.

Step 2: Copy the comments from the decision letter

Step 3: Reply the comments one-by-one. For each comment,

Answer the question precisely

List the modifications in response to the comment
