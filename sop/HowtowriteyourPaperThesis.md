**How to write your Paper/Thesis?**

1. Outline of a Paper
    1. Title: Summarize the main theme of this paper
    2. Abstract: Summarize the contributions of the paper.
    3. Introduction: Essential background knowledge required to understand this paper
        1. Background knowledge related to your Title
            1. WHAT is the problem?
            2. WHY this problem is important?
        2. Background knowledge related to your Problem
            1. WHAT are the technical terms?
            2. WHAT is the challenge in dealing with the problem?
        3. Background knowledge related to your Solution
            1. WHAT are the related works?
            2. HOW does each related work deal with similar problems?
            3. WHAT is the limitation of existing solution?
        4. Contributions of this paper.
    2. System Model/Architecture: Problem Definition
        1. WHAT is the problem?
        2. WHAT are the I/P and O/P parameters in defining the problem (e.g., network topology/timing diagram/block diagram, etc.)?
        3. WHAT is the physical meaning or relationship among the I/P and O/P parameters (i.e., use a figure to define it)
        4. WHAT are the assumptions? HOW/WHERE does each assumption affect your results?
    3. Proposed Method:
        1. WHAT is your solution?
        2. HOW does your solution works?
        3. HOW do you implement your concepts?
        4. HOW can the others use your solution?
    4. Numerical/Simulation/Experimental Results:
        1. Simulation/Experimental scenarios considered in this paper
            1. WHAT is the purpose of each scenario (i.e., verify the effectiveness of your contribution, statistics showing your finding)
            2. HOW do you design each scenario (topologies, fixed v.s. configurable parameters, etc.)?
            3. WHAT are the expected results (X- and Y-axis of the figures)?
        2. Observations
            1. WHAT do you find from your results?
    5. Conclusion and Future Works
2. Basic requirements for your thesis
3. Steps required to write your paper
    1. 1st Draft (immediately after fixing the thesis topic; repeat it AGAIN if you decide to change your thesis topic):
        1. Contribution in Introduction
        2. Simulation/Experimental scenarios and expected results
        3. System Model /Architecture
    2. 2nd Draft
        1. Finish the ‘Related Works’ of Introduction.
        2. Follow the template of the paper to summarize
