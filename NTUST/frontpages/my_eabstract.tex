In the nFAPI (Option 6) disaggregated RAN architecture, physical separation of the MAC (VNF) and PHY (PNF) over packet-switched networks introduces critical timing synchronization issues. To prevent message rejection at the PNF timing window, the VNF must schedule and transmit control messages ahead of time. This thesis proposes an adaptive timing control framework that dynamically adjusts this scheduling lead time (slots ahead) using closed-loop feedback based on real-time Timing Info reports from the PNF. By continuously updating the slots ahead, the proposed method automatically adapts to diverse deployment conditions, including fluctuating network traffic (offered load), latency, and jitter. Experimental validation on an OpenAirInterface testbed integrated with a commercial Radio Unit (O-RU) demonstrates that the mechanism eliminates packet drop errors, stabilizes round-trip times (RTT), and maintains stable throughput even under severe network congestion.
